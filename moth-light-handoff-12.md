### Next: land revision (the user's idea, not built; confirm before building)
The user wants the moth to fly over land, not through space. This is an explicit **exception** to the background brief's "no ground, horizon or seabed" rule, which was written for the four space stages. Proposed approach (my suggestion, not agreed):
- Keep the screen top-down: terrain scrolls down beneath the moth as if flying over it. No side-view horizon (it clashes with falling hazards).
- Remove the stars and the dust-in-space look. The ground is a cached tiled terrain texture of banded, stretched noise (grass green, dirt ochre, stone teal), scrolled with a few `drawImage` calls; heat stretches it along y and tears and snaps it as now.
- Slabs become raised mesas and ridge walls with a drop shadow (a height cue); long thin stretched ridges and streaks at high heat are the "Farlands" read. Add slow cloud shadows or haze, a soft shadow under the moth, and a sunlit sky tint at the horizon-less edges.
- Seam becomes a terrain cliff wall, shear a landslide slab. Hazards stay violet and orbs stay bright.
- Stage card art: land seen from above with a seam wall and the moth, replacing the floating slabs.
- Palette stays close to `PAL[5]` but brighter in the middle. Keep sprites cached and low-effects halving counts.

## Edge strips (v40-v43; the user's decisions, do not undo without asking)
The scene is wider than the screen with a hidden strip on each side (layout 16 | 68 | 16 %); dragging the moth to the edge slides and banks the camera; **no drawn guide**, players discover the strips (hints only from drifting loot, a panned chime, a subtle light bleed); rubber-band resistance, per-stage strip flavour and per-stage rubber band; **strips are high risk, high reward, keep hazards, no fairness aids**; jackpot items; the camera must feel silky, not snappy; the background is one continuous full map; **lightning and every full-width hazard must cover the strips, a strip is never a refuge**; loot flies on a smooth S-curve.
- **Coordinates:** world x: the stage is [0, W], strips are [-stripW, 0] and [W, W+stripW]; `xL()/xR()` outer edges, `wLo()/wHi()` the moth's limits (24 px inside). **Screen x = world x - `EC.x`.** `EC = { x, xv, roll, rv, gl, gr, hl, hr }`. `EP[stage] = { s, c, r }` (rubber stiffness, camera spring frequency, roll strength): .55/.6/.5/.72/.55 stiffness for stages 0-4, Stage 6 is `{ .6, 7.5, 1.1 }`.
- **Steering:** `point()` stores `fingerSX`; `ecAim()` sets `moth.tx`. Inside the edge zone (12% of W) the finger to moth mapping has resistance; moth chase is multiplied by `rub = 1 - .45*|EC.x|/stripW`. **Camera `ecStep`:** a critically damped spring (about .6 s to settle, no overshoot); the moth rides along. **Roll:** `-(EC.x/stripW)*.052*EP.r` rad through an under-damped spring; reduce-motion turns roll off but keeps the pan.
- **Rendering:** `ecApply(par)` rotates about the moth then translates by `-EC.x*par`; the world layer sits inside a `ctx.save()` in `draw()`, the HUD stays fixed. **Full-map background:** `drawBG(t, pulse, slide)` widens `W` by `2*stripW()` and sets `bgS`; background elements live in B coordinates (world x + stripW); `update()` spawns the background over the whole width; `wBase` holds the real W while widened (the `catch` in `frame()` restores it). **Anything new in the background must treat W as the world width and use `bgS` as the stage offset.** **Oversize every full-height or full-width effect** (-H*.35 .. H*1.35, -W .. 2W) or the banked camera shows its ends. `drawStripDeco(t)` draws per-stage flavour inside the strips (Moonlit fireflies, Nebula bubbles, Aurora crystals, Crimson embers, Neon pixels, Stage 6 flecks). `drawEdgeBleed(t)` paints a 54 px light bleed at the screen edge (gold loot to red hazards, max alpha .14).
- **Loot and hazards in strips:** `edgeDirector(g)` spawns loot lanes every `lerp(7.5, 5.2, diff)` s (jackpot after `jackCD` 24 s, then 36-56 s; 30 x multiplier, +8 heat, a 6-orb cascade); strip hazards every `lerp(3.4, 1.5, diff)` s per strip (Nebula spore 50%, Aurora shard 55%, Crimson lightning lane 42%, else a shuriken with `strip: true`); a **linger comet** if the moth stays deeper than 55% of a strip for `lerp(3.6, 2.2, diff)` s. Full-width events cover the strips; gates, loading-bar gaps and countdown safe lanes are always on the stage.
- **Not built from the idea list, do not add without asking:** edge streak multiplier, cross bonus, dark strips lit by the moth, edge collectibles that unlock gear, boss reward drops, pocket power-ups.

## Systems reference
- **Stage flow:** `startTransition()` picks the target at random (never the current stage, or forced by the Test button) into `trans.to`; `stageSwap()` applies it. `stageNo` counts jumps; `STAGE_STEP = 250` (`score >= (stageNo+1)*250`); hazard speed +8% per full cycle of `STAGES.length` jumps. Non-Neon transition 4.6 s (`swapAt` 1.8), Neon jumps use the Y2K glitch plus Matrix rain; invulnerable `trans.dur + .3`. The jump is deferred while a boss is active or a cutscene runs.
- **Six stages (index = array slot):** Moonlit Garden 0 (`reset` heat), Nebula Reef 1 (`decay`), Aurora Sea 2 (`overdrive`), Crimson Storm 3 (`inverse`), Neon Mirage 4 (`glitch`), Overflow Reach 5 (`drift`). Heat names: Moonlit Dusk, Glimmer, Firefly, Moonblaze, Full Moon | Nebula Still Water, Ripple, Bloom, Riptide, Maelstrom | Aurora Frost, Shimmer, Aurora, Solar Wind, Polar Surge | Crimson Overcast, Rumble, Squall, Tempest, Cataclysm | Neon Sunset, Neon Haze, Static, Glitch, System Crash | Overflow Origin, Drift, Stretch, Shear, Overflow. `STAGES[i]` fields: `name, heatNames, tip, heat, gain, shMul, extra, mult, events[], powers[], boss{kind,name,hp} or null`.
- **Keep these index-aligned with `STAGES` (six entries now):** `PAL, SH_STYLE, CM_STYLE, OSTYLE, EP, POPF, ART_SKY, ART`, the `padE` array in `drawPopText`, the colour array in `drawHole`, `uw`, `save.seen`. **`TH` (background thunder) has only 5 entries; Stage 6 skips thunder on purpose.** `BG_TABLES` has four (Neon and Stage 6 have their own scenery). A missing entry crashes (a `TH` miss crashed the first v52 run); an earlier chat once put a `TH` entry in the wrong slot.
- **Health bar** (v31): `hp` 0-100; `hitHazard(x, y, kind)` rolls `rollDmg(kind)` from `DMG` (comet 2-4, shard 2-3, bullet 2-4, shuriken 4-6, spore 4-6, hole 6-9, lbar 6-9, popup 5-8, owl 4-7, **shear 6-10**, strike 8-12, gate 8-12, marq 10-14, xsweep 15-20). 1.5 s invulnerability, no regeneration; moonbeam heal +25%, boss kill +20%; shield and Ctrl+Z block fully.
- **Heat and score:** heat 0-1 drives speed, spawns, shuriken count, wobble, homing; `HEAT_MULT` 1/1.3/1.6/2/2.5, Nebula 1/1.5/2/2.6/3.2, Stage 6 1/1.4/1.9/2.5/3.2; streak of 5 orbs +.1 up to +.5. Heat rules in short: Moonlit a hit resets heat; Nebula drains by itself, orbs 1.6x, a hit x0.4; Aurora max heat gives 9 s Overdrive (invincible, smash hazards, orbs x4) then 25%; Crimson grows by itself exponentially, orbs cool 3%, a hit +25%, max gives a Storm Surge of 7 strikes then 35%; Neon Glitch (a hit resets heat; from .5 shurikens jump sideways with a telegraph, from .75 they flicker); Stage 6 drift (above).
- **Events:** `runEvent` picks from `stage().events`, gated by `need` heat thresholds (Stage 6: seam .1, shear .15, shower .2, gate .25, hole .45...), one big screen event at a time (`heavyOn()`), every 7-14 s, faster with heat. Core events: comet (warning lane tracks the moth, locks the last .35 s, dives), gate, hole, shower, spore, shard, strike lanes, Moonlit owl, Nebula current, Aurora ice, Crimson cloud, Neon popup/marq/lbar/countdown, Stage 6 seam/shear. `CLR` lists item types that nova/defrag clear.
- **Power-ups** (about 8% of spawns, 10-16 s cooldown, pool of 6 per stage): shield, magnet (8 s), slow (6 s, 55% speed), nova (clears, 8 boss damage), bloom (hazards to orbs), prism (6 s beam), chill (-40% heat), heal (+25% health), double (10 s x2), warm (+40%), freeze (4 s), boost (Overdrive), phase (4 s ghost), rod (8 s, absorbs lightning), eye (6 s, heat growth and events pause), Neon's ctrlz, defrag, dialup, save. **Reseed (planned for Stage 6):** pulls heat back and snaps nearby hazards onto the grid.
- **Bosses:** appear at heat level 2, rematch at level 4 (+14 HP), cooldown 40 s after a defeat, 30 s after a retreat, forced 55 s into a stage, retreat after 140 s. HP is `round((def.hp + level*14) * 4)`; three phases at 66% and 33% health (staggered 3 s, x3 damage; armor drained before health; weak-point window with x2 damage); `CINE` intro (2.9 s) and defeat (2.8 s) cutscenes; skippable. Kinds: Void Eye (Moonlit), Spore Mother, Prism Sentinel, Storm Titan, Neon Sentinel. Stage 6 has none until step 4.
- **Pause** (button, P/Esc, auto on `visibilitychange`); Sound on/off button bottom-left; `reduceMotion` respected; unlocks and gear use `localStorage` key `mothlight.gear` (wrapped in try/catch).
- **Audio:** all WebAudio synthesis, layers build with heat; per-stage style, key and tempo; `MASTER 1.1`, `MUSIC 1.8`, compressor, voice lines at `VRATE .93` (the narrator line "Warning! System's breaking." at Neon heat .7). `Sound.setStage(i)` picks the style (`i === 4 ? 4 : i % 4`, so Stage 6 currently plays Nebula's).
- **Gotchas:** never reassign `items` or `bolts` while looping (nova and bloom are queued until after the item loop); `glow()` multiplies the current alpha (how the HUD fades); item types: orb, gold, shuriken (including boss bullets), power, comet, gate (including seams), hole, spore, shard, strike, shear, owl, cloud, popup and the other event items.
